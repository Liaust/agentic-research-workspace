# Public roadmap

This roadmap describes the intended direction of Agentic Research Workspace;
it is not a promise of timing or accepted implementation scope.

## Surface 1: literature map

This is the implemented surface today.

- Improve contributor documentation and add small, rights-safe fixtures for
  schema and relationship-mapping work.
- Expand reproducible verification examples for aggregate baselines and
  synthetic graphs without publishing private evidence-bearing inputs.
- Measure source-reading fidelity, retrievability, and cross-source calibration
  against frozen benchmarks.
- Improve layout-aware treatment of equations, figures, tables, references,
  and meaningful document structure.
- Strengthen quality-state visibility and recovery of unresolved or
  structurally invalid sources.
- Test graph navigation and comparison interfaces without collapsing
  disagreement or provenance.

## Surface 2: research workbench

Planned work will explore how researchers and agents can use one explicit
project state to:

- identify literature gaps and develop questions worth investigating;
- connect evolving notes, decisions, datasets, experiments, and code;
- preserve provenance and supersession as ideas change; and
- automate bounded workflows without hiding inputs, state, or review gates.

## Surface 3: research pack

Planned work will define a portable, inspectable publication that exposes the
research corpus and its provenance to humans and agents. It should complement,
not merely reproduce, a conventional paper or PDF.

## Publication boundaries

A release of source-derived data needs a dedicated rights assessment, dataset
scope, license, and manifest. Each future feature, release, deployment, and
announcement remains a separate action with its own scope and validation.
